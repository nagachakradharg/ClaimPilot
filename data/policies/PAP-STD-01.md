# Part A — General provisions

### [GEN-1] Policy period and territory
This policy applies only to accidents and losses that occur during the policy period shown in the Declarations and within the United States, its territories or possessions, or Canada.

### [GEN-2] Definitions
"You" means the named insured shown in the Declarations. "Your covered auto" means the vehicle described in the Declarations. "Loss" means direct and accidental damage to, or theft of, your covered auto, including its equipment. "Actual cash value" means the replacement cost of the vehicle less depreciation at the time of loss.

### [GEN-3] Fraud
We do not provide coverage for any insured who has made fraudulent statements or engaged in fraudulent conduct in connection with any accident or loss for which coverage is sought. Any such determination is made by a claims professional after investigation.

# Part D — Coverage for damage to your auto

### [COLL-1] Collision coverage
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto caused by collision, minus the collision deductible shown in the Declarations. "Collision" means the upset of your covered auto or its impact with another vehicle or object, including a parked vehicle, a pole, a guardrail, or a building.

### [COMP-1] Other than collision (comprehensive) coverage
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto not caused by collision, minus the comprehensive deductible shown in the Declarations. Loss caused by the following is considered other than collision: missiles or falling objects; fire; theft or larceny; explosion or earthquake; windstorm; hail, water or flood; malicious mischief or vandalism; riot or civil commotion; contact with a bird or animal; or breakage of glass.

### [COMP-2] Glass breakage
If breakage of glass is caused by a collision, you may elect to have it treated as loss caused by collision. Repair of a windshield chip without replacement is paid with no deductible.

### [COMP-3] Theft of the entire vehicle
For theft of your covered auto, you must report the theft to the police within 48 hours of discovering it. We will pay for loss only after 30 days have passed without recovery of the vehicle, unless the vehicle is recovered earlier in damaged condition.

### [LIM-1] Limit of liability
Our limit of liability for loss to your covered auto is the lesser of: (a) the actual cash value of the damaged or stolen property; or (b) the amount necessary to repair or replace the property with other property of like kind and quality. An adjustment for depreciation and physical condition is made in determining actual cash value at the time of loss.

### [LIM-2] Total loss
If the cost to repair your covered auto is 75% or more of its actual cash value, we will treat it as a total loss and pay the actual cash value minus the applicable deductible.

### [DED-1] Deductibles
The collision deductible and comprehensive deductible are shown in the Declarations and are applied separately to each loss. Only one deductible applies when a single event causes both collision and other than collision loss.

# Part D — Exclusions

### [EXC-1] Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown
We will not pay for loss due to and confined to wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown or failure, or road damage to tires, unless the damage results from the total theft of your covered auto.

### [EXC-2] Commercial use and ride-sharing
We will not pay for loss to your covered auto while it is being used to carry persons or property for a fee, including while the driver is logged in to a transportation network or delivery platform. This exclusion does not apply to shared-expense car pools.

### [EXC-3] Racing
We will not pay for loss to your covered auto while it is located inside a facility designed for racing, for the purpose of competing in, practicing or preparing for any prearranged or organized racing or speed contest.

### [EXC-4] Intentional damage
We will not pay for loss caused intentionally by or at the direction of you or any family member.

### [EXC-5] Unlicensed or excluded driver
We will not pay for loss sustained while your covered auto is being operated by a person who does not hold a valid driver's license, or by a person specifically excluded in the Declarations.

### [EXC-6] Custom equipment and personal property
We will not pay for loss to non-permanently installed electronic equipment, or to personal property left in the vehicle, such as phones, laptops or luggage. Aftermarket equipment above $1,000 is covered only if listed in the Declarations.

### [EXC-7] Nuclear, war, and confiscation
We will not pay for loss due to war, insurrection, nuclear reaction or radiation, or confiscation by governmental or civil authorities.

# Part E — Duties after an accident or loss

### [DUT-1] Notice and cooperation
We must be notified promptly of how, when and where the accident or loss happened, normally within 30 days. You must cooperate with us in the investigation, submit proof of loss when required, and allow us to inspect and appraise the damaged property before its repair or disposal.

### [DUT-2] Protecting the vehicle
You must take reasonable steps after loss to protect your covered auto and its equipment from further loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred to do this.
