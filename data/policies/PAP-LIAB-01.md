# Part A — General provisions

### [GEN-1] Policy period and territory
This policy applies only to accidents and losses that occur during the policy period shown in the Declarations and within the United States, its territories or possessions, or Canada.

### [GEN-2] Definitions
"You" means the named insured shown in the Declarations. "Your covered auto" means the vehicle described in the Declarations. "Property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property owned by others.

# Part B — Liability coverage

### [LIAB-1] Insuring agreement
We will pay damages for bodily injury or property damage to others for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident, up to the liability limits shown in the Declarations. We will settle or defend any claim or suit asking for these damages.

### [LIAB-2] Damage to your own vehicle not covered
This policy provides liability coverage only. It does not include Collision or Other Than Collision (comprehensive) coverage. We will not pay for loss to your covered auto from any cause, including collision, theft, fire, vandalism, weather, or glass breakage.

### [LIAB-3] Exclusions
We do not provide liability coverage for any insured who intentionally causes bodily injury or property damage, or while the vehicle is used to carry persons or property for a fee.

# Part E — Duties after an accident or loss

### [DUT-1] Notice and cooperation
We must be notified promptly of how, when and where the accident or loss happened, normally within 30 days. You must cooperate with us in the investigation and send us copies of any legal papers received in connection with the accident.
